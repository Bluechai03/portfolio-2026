# Portfolio 2026

## Users
- Hiring managers and engineers who screen product-engineer candidates, mostly in the UAE and for remote roles. They skim for two to five minutes, usually on a laptop, sometimes on a phone from a recruiter's link, and decide whether to read further.
- Recruiters who forward the link to a hiring manager. They need the role, the years of experience and one live project in the first screen.
- Anna Montero, who edits the site and adds projects and case studies as she builds them.

## Product
- Next.js site with a design system, e2e tests (Playwright) and unit tests (Vitest), deployed on Vercel, in the repo portfolio-2026.
- Projects carry a status: Idea, Planned, In progress or Live. Case studies tell the problem, the decisions and what comes next.
- Live project: Strut, the interval walking timer (PWA, separate repo). Planned: class booking app, audio sleep project.
- No revenue model.

## Constraints
- Free tiers only for the site and every project it links to.
- Nothing from the current employer appears on the site: no name, screenshots, code or metrics.
- Sentence case for every heading, eyebrow, badge and label.
- Copy is warm and kind; commands and blunt phrasing are rewritten.
- Accessibility is claimed only for what an audit has proven.
- Anna merges pull requests; the agent opens them.

## Ethical stance
- No analytics that identify a visitor; no cookie banner needed.
- Claims on the site are checkable: every skill, metric and "live" status points to something a visitor can open.
- No Fabricated Scarcity or Fake Social Proof: no invented testimonials, user counts or availability banners.
- Opt-in for any contact or newsletter capture; none at launch.

## Success
- The target role is product engineer, mid-level.
- A hiring manager reaches a live project and a case study within two minutes of landing and can say what Anna builds and which decisions she made.
- Interviews come from the site: a recruiter or hiring manager replies after viewing it.
