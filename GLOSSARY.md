# Portfolio 2026

Anna Montero's portfolio site: proof of what she builds, aimed at people hiring product engineers.

## Language

**Project**:
Something Anna builds and shows on the site as proof of her work.
_Avoid_: Work item, piece, ship

**Case study**:
A page that tells the story of one project: the problem, the decisions behind it, and what comes next.
_Avoid_: Write-up, deep dive, project page

**Status**:
Where a project stands right now: Idea, Planned, In progress or Live.
_Avoid_: Ongoing, Shipped, Done

**Idea**:
A project Anna hasn't committed to building yet.

**Planned**:
A project Anna has committed to but not started.

**In progress**:
A project being built that isn't publicly usable yet.

**Live**:
A project anyone can use publicly, even if work on it continues.
_Avoid_: Shipped, Released, Done

**Sentence case**:
The style for every heading, eyebrow, badge and label on the site: capitalise the first word and proper nouns only.
_Avoid_: Title Case, ALL CAPS
